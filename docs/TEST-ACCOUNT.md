## Niche and persona
- Niche: relatable human behaviour / psychology ("signs you're the type of person
  who…", "things nobody tells you about…", "if you do this it means…").
- Persona: early-twenties, faceless, moody aesthetic stills (rain on a window, empty
  late-night kitchens, city at dusk), quiet first-person observations. No face, no
  voice, no product.
- Why (VSC minus convertibility): the most repeated faceless format among small
  accounts; saves/shares are the native reaction; passes "this is me" by design; one
  generated image per post fits the budget.

## Phases and gates (playbook §4)
- Day 1–2 (Sep 2–3): lurker. 3–4 sessions/day, no posts, no comments, ≤1 follow/session.
- Day 3–4: training. Niche searches each session ("psychology facts", "signs you",
  "things nobody tells you", "human behaviour", "overthinking"), watch top results
  through, like, ≤1 plain comment/session. Goal: feed ~70% niche, US creators.
- Day 4–5: health test. Recreate one top single-image+text post from the niche
  (image regenerated, EXACT same overlay text and caption). Wait 24–48 h.
  ≥700 views → go. 300–700 → one more warm-up post. <300 → account is dead; tell Josh.
- Posting phase: 1/day for the first 8 (each a distinct hypothesis), then 2/day
  scaling whatever cleared 2k. Golden windows only (6–8, 10–12, 17–20 local).
